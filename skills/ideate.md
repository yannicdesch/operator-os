# Ideate

1. Restate the problem in one sentence and name the priority in `me.md` it serves.
2. Generate at least five genuinely different approaches, including one that builds nothing (process, pricing or policy change) and one that is deliberately small.
3. Stress-test each: cost, time to first evidence, risk, conflict with any closed decision or constraint in `company.md`, who in `people.md` would resist.
4. Cut to a shortlist of two or three with a one-line reason for each cut.
5. Recommend one, and say what you would need to learn to be sure. Offer opos-pitch or opos-spec as the next step.
